OpenShift Application Lifecycle Workshop

This session is planned. Its content will be finalised after the previous sessions, based on the applications and questions the team brings.

Decide how many environments to run, how they are governed, and how GitOps is organised across clusters.

Where it fits

Lifecycle stage Builds on

Platform

The hardened notes-app from Session 1 and the admission checklist

Planned steps

  1. Environment strategy: production-only clusters, pre-production and disposable test clusters

  2. Apply governance policies to several clusters from one hub

  3. Compare one Argo CD per cluster with a central Argo CD

  4. Test platform changes (operators, upgrades) before they reach production

  5. Record the decisions in a decision log

What the session leaves behind

  • Decision log entries for environments and GitOps topology

  • A starter set of governance policies

Questions to prepare

  • What should be the role of the management cluster?

  • Which changes must be tested outside production first?
