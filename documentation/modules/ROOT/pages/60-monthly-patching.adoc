IMPORTANT: This session is planned. Its content will be finalised after the previous sessions, based on the applications and questions the team brings.

Make the monthly base image update a routine: detect a new base image, rebuild, scan, redeploy through GitOps and verify, with clear ownership between platform and application teams.

== Planned steps

. Track which base image digest every workload runs
. Detect a newer base image and trigger a rebuild automatically
. Scan the new image and compare vulnerabilities with the running one
. Promote the new digest through Git and roll out with zero downtime
. Verify, and roll back if needed
. Define who patches what: platform-built images, vendor images and the cluster itself

== What the session leaves behind

* A documented monthly patch workflow
* A responsibility split between platform and application teams

== Questions to prepare

* What is the required patch cadence, and what evidence must be produced?
* How quickly must vendors deliver patched images?
