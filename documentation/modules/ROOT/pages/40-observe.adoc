= Session 4 - Run and Observe: Metrics, Alerts and Logs That Scale

IMPORTANT: This session is planned. Its content will be finalised after the previous sessions, based on the applications and questions the team brings.

Give application teams visibility into their own workloads, and keep log volumes under control before they reach the central log platform.

== Where it fits

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Lifecycle stage | Builds on

| Run
| The hardened `notes-app` from xref:15-stateless-and-scale.adoc[Session 1] and the xref:16-admission-checklist.adoc[admission checklist]
|===

== Planned steps

. Enable user workload monitoring and scrape application metrics with a ServiceMonitor
. Create a PrometheusRule alert for the application and route it to the owning team
. Explore application logs in the console
. Forward logs with a ClusterLogForwarder, filtering and dropping noise before export
. Agree on what the platform team monitors and what application teams monitor

== What the session leaves behind

* An observability baseline for every tenant
* A log filtering and forwarding pattern

== Questions to prepare

* Which log destinations are in use, and what volume is acceptable?
* Who receives application alerts today?
